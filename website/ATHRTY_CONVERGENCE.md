# ATHRTY / Vercel-to-Hatchable Experience Contract

## Scope
Vercel website/ is the ATHRTY company flagship. Hatchable owns product flows. The private Cash Holdings OS is not a public route.

## Canonical brand and routing
Obsidian Mint is the source of visual truth: #070e12 canvas, #0d171c panels, #14262d raised, #8cefe5 mint, 18px card radii. Shared routes: /, /platform, /products, /solutions, /work, /company, /contact. Studio/login redirect explicitly to Hatchable.

## Responsive release gates
Width | Layout | Acceptance
--- | --- | ---

375 | Phone | Menu and accessible focus; content wraps

768 | Tablet portrait | Drawer, single-column hero
1024 | Tablet landscape | Navigation and one visible CTA
1440 | Desktop | Two-column hero, three-column cards
1920 | Wide | Content max-width, readable measures

## Governance
The static Vercel site does not own payments, subscriptions, tenant authorization, personal customer data, or native project publishing. A green build is not equivalent to device, screen-reader, Web Vitals or authenticated workflow certification.

## Follow-up engineering
Extract the token palette as a versioned shared artifact, make route ownership explicit in both frontends, and certify sign-in and product deep-link handoffs without assuming cross-domain sessions. Keep the governance repository and private operating data intact.
